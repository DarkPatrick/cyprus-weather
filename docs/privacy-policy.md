# Privacy policy — Kairo: Cyprus Weather

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Kairo: Cyprus Weather ("Kairo", "the app") is a free weather app for Cyprus, developed and operated by Egor Semin. This policy explains what data the app and its server handle. Contact for any privacy question or request: [egor.semin.cy@gmail.com](mailto:egor.semin.cy@gmail.com).

Kairo has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share personal data.

## Data the app uses

**Device location.** With your permission, the app reads your device location to pick the nearest weather station and, when the GPS fix is precise, your elevation above sea level for the pressure reading. Your coordinates are processed on the device and are never sent to our server. Location is read only while the app is open, never in the background. You can deny the permission and choose a station by hand.

**Weather requests.** To load the weather, the app sends the Kairo server the code of the selected weather station, the elevation in whole metres (when available from GPS) and the interface language. The server uses them only to build the response, keeps responses in memory for up to two minutes to serve repeated requests, and does not write them to logs or storage.

**Anonymous app opens.** On first launch the app creates a random identifier stored on the device. Each time the app is opened (or reopened after at least 30 minutes in the background) it sends this identifier to our server, which stores it with the time of the visit. This is used only to count daily usage. The identifier is not linked to your name, account, device identifiers, advertising ID, location or IP address. Records are deleted automatically after 24 months. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling the app creates a new identifier.

**Settings on the device.** The selected station, language and theme are stored only on your device.

**Server logs.** Our web server does not keep access logs for the app's API. The server's network layer necessarily sees your IP address while handling a request, but Kairo does not store it.

## Third-party services contacted by the app

When you use certain features, your device connects directly to these services, which receive your IP address and standard browser headers under their own privacy policies:

- **OpenStreetMap tile servers** (OpenStreetMap Foundation), to display the station map — [privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- **Cyprus Department of Meteorology website** (dom.org.cy), to display the official forecast map and table images.

Weather, air-quality and lightning data from Open-Meteo, Copernicus (CAMS), the Cyprus Department of Meteorology, the Department of Labour Inspection, MeteoAlarm and EUMETSAT are collected by our server. Your device does not contact those providers and no user data is sent to them.

## Children

Kairo is a general-audience weather app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Your choices and requests

You can deny or revoke the location permission at any time in the system settings. Because app-open records are anonymous, we cannot link them to you; clearing the app's data starts a new identifier. For any question or request, write to [egor.semin.cy@gmail.com](mailto:egor.semin.cy@gmail.com).

## Security

All communication between the app and the Kairo server uses HTTPS.

## Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when the policy changes.
