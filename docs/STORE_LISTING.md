# Store listing (Google Play / App Store)

**App name:** Kairo: Cyprus Weather

**Short description (≤ 80 characters):**
Live Cyprus weather stations, sea, air quality, Saharan dust and forecasts.

**Full description:**

Kairo shows the weather in Cyprus as it is measured: live readings from weather stations across the island, with the nearest station picked automatically.

• Temperature, feels-like (UTCI), wind, pressure, humidity and rain from official stations
• Sea temperature and marine forecasts
• Air quality: PM2.5, PM10, ozone, NO₂, SO₂, CO, the European Air Quality Index and Saharan dust
• Sun position, sunrise and sunset, UV index
• Official forecasts and weather warnings, plus a short AI summary
• Station map with the last 24 hours, lightning and rain
• English, Greek and Russian

Free, no ads, no account.

Data sources: Cyprus Department of Meteorology (observations, sea, forecasts and warnings), Department of Labour Inspection (air-quality measurements), Open-Meteo (weather model, CC BY 4.0), Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (air-quality and UV model), MeteoAlarm (warnings), EUMETSAT (lightning), © OpenStreetMap contributors (map).

Kairo is an independent app. It is not affiliated with and does not represent the Cyprus Department of Meteorology, the Department of Labour Inspection or any other government body.

## Data safety (Google Play)

- Data collected: **Device or other IDs** (anonymous install ID) and **App activity → App interactions** (app opens) — not shared, not linked to identity, used for Analytics; **Approximate location** (selected station and elevation sent with weather requests) — processed ephemerally, not stored, used for App functionality.
- Precise location: read on the device only, not transmitted → not "collected".
- Data encrypted in transit: yes. Deletion request: via the contact email (records are anonymous and expire after 24 months).
- Target audience: 13+ (not designed for children).
